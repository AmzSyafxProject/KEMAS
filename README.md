**KEMAS is a premium, native Windows utility that keeps your computer clean, organized and fast — without the clutter, ads or complexity of traditional cleaners.**

Every computer slows down over time. Temporary files pile up, caches grow silently, downloads turn into chaos and background apps quietly consume memory. KEMAS brings it all back under control in a single, beautifully crafted experience. One click restores space and speed; thoughtful safeguards make sure nothing important is ever touched.

Built natively for Windows 10 and 11, KEMAS renders at your display's full refresh rate, runs every heavy task in the background so the interface never stutters, and releases its own memory when idle. It is light enough to forget about, and powerful enough to rely on every day.

### What KEMAS does
- **Smart Clean** — one click to clear temporary files, browser caches, Prefetch, thumbnail caches, error reports, update leftovers and the Recycle Bin, then free up unused memory.
- **Health Score** — a live overview of storage, memory and junk, so you always know how your PC is doing.
- **File Organizer** — sorts Downloads, Pictures, Videos, Music and any folder you choose into tidy folders by type or by date. Your rules, your folder names. Export your setup and share it with anyone. Every move can be undone.
- **Memory Booster** — see exactly which apps use your RAM, release unused memory, or close heavy apps safely. Windows system processes are always protected.
- **Startup Apps** — choose what launches with Windows for a faster boot.
- **Programs** — find the biggest programs on your PC and remove the ones you no longer need.

### Designed with care
- Elegant dark and light themes, or follow Windows automatically
- English and Bahasa Melayu
- Automatic cleaning on a daily or weekly schedule
- Built-in installer, Start menu shortcuts and clean uninstall
- Secure automatic updates, verified with SHA-256 before installation
- Private by design no accounts, no tracking, no ads

### Get started
1. Download **KEMAS.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/amzarsyaf2002/KEMAS/releases/latest).
2. Run it and choose **Yes** when Windows asks for permission.
3. Choose **Install** to add KEMAS to your Start menu — future updates arrive automatically inside the app.
